  <INFERENCES>
    <INFERENCE>A hypothetical extractor that, after the prefix, shifts only selected valid words into a 16-word accumulator while suppressing zero-run words cannot be correct using only whole 128-bit-per-lane / 8-word beat enables for the full single-decimation range: f2 requires a per-word mask or equivalent partial append at both offset1 boundaries. Whole-beat enables suffice for the source boundaries of f0, f1 and f3, but that does not cover f2.</INFERENCE>
    <INFERENCE>The f2 partial boundary is the worst case across N=1..63: it requires retaining exactly one selected word in one boundary beat and exactly seven selected words in the next. A design whose only state update unit is all-or-nothing 8 words necessarily either discards a valid word or admits zero padding there.</INFERENCE>
    <INFERENCE>DDC must be preserved as an independent schedule, including its I/Q phase and source-layout zero positions. This evidence does not establish that a whole-beat-only masked-append mechanism is safe for all DDC fractions; v1.12’s all-beat history plus positional block slicing avoids relying on that unproven simplification.</INFERENCE>
  </INFERENCES>
