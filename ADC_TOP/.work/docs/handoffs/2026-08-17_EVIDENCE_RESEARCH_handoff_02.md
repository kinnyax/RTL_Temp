    <FACT confidence="HIGH">The register manual defines two physical register fields, not a raw 8-bit DEC_M device register. CFG_DEMOD_FACT_FRAC at offset 0x09 has [1:0] cfg_demod_fact_frac: 00=F0, 01=F0.25, 10=F0.5, 11=F0.75 (physical page 66 / printed 59). Its [8] selects profile_dec_frac (0) versus cfg_demod_dec_frac (1); [7:2] is reserved.</FACT>
    <FACT confidence="HIGH">CFG_DEMOD_FACT_INT at offset 0x0A has [5:0] cfg_demod_fact_int with range [1,63] and field reset 0x02 (physical page 67 / printed 60). The register’s total reset is 0x0002 (page 66). Its [8] selects profile_int_frac (0) versus cfg_demod_int_frac (1), and [7:6] is reserved (page 66).</FACT>
    <FACT confidence="HIGH">Therefore the documented factor is M=N+F, with N=1..63 and F in {0, .25, .5, .75}; it spans 1.00 through 63.75 in .25 steps. The same representation/range appears in the grouping workbook sheet3 B2. “63.75 maximum” is the exact endpoint implied by these documented field ranges.</FACT>
    <FACT confidence="HIGH">A deliberate ADC-side compact control E={N[5:0],f[1:0]}=4*N+f is a lossless encoding of these two factor fields. With every documented N/F combination enabled, E is exactly 8'h04 through 8'hFF inclusive; 8'hFF represents 63.75. This is an internal packed-control convention, not the AC9810 raw register map and does not carry either physical register’s bit-[8] profile/direct selection.</FACT>
    <FACT confidence="HIGH">The 2-CML framing zero-run table is identical for pure decimation (sheet3) and decimation+DDC (sheet4).
