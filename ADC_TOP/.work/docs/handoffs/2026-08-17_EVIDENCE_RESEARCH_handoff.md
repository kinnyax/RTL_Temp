  <FACTS>
    <FACT confidence="HIGH">The vendor configuration workbook defines the physical decimation configuration as two distinct registers, not one 8-bit register: CFG_DEMOD_FACT_FRAC at 0x509 (C24/D24) and CFG_DEMOD_FACT_INT at 0x50A (C25/D25).</FACT>
    <FACT confidence="HIGH">CFG_DEMOD_FACT_FRAC[1:0] encodes F exactly as 0=&gt;0, 1=&gt;0.25, 2=&gt;0.5, 3=&gt;0.75; its bit [8] selects profile versus direct-register source (I24). CFG_DEMOD_FACT_INT[5:0] encodes integer N with stated range [1,63], and its bit [8] likewise selects profile versus direct-register source (I25). Thus the documented factor is M=N+F, has 0.25 steps, and documented mathematical endpoints are 1.00 through 63.75. “Maximum 63.75” follows explicitly from the stated N/F ranges; it is not a separately printed maximum phrase.</FACT>
    <FACT confidence="HIGH">A packed firmware representation E=4*N+f, f=FACT_FRAC[1:0], would numerically range 4..255 and represent 63.75 as 8'hFF. This is an arithmetic equivalence only: the source does not name E/DEC_M, does not define one 8-bit device field, and does not remove the two source-select bits [8].</FACT>
    <FACT confidence="HIGH">The grouping workbook independently states (sheet 3 B2) M=N+F, N=1..63, F in {0,0.25,0.5,0.75}. It is therefore corroboration for the separate-register encoding/range, including 63.75.</FACT>
    <FACT confidence="HIGH">For both pure decimation (sheet 3) and decimation+DDC (sheet 4), the 2-CML zero-run table is identical. For no discard it is: N.0: 16N+152 (T41 / T40); N.25: 64N+168 (T44 / T43); N.5: 32N+168 (T47 / T46); N.75: 64N+200 (T50 / T49). The 4-CML and 8-CML forms are also stated at M/F and F/F respectively in those same rows.</FACT>
    <FACT confidence="HIGH">The same tables define discard modes in their C/J/Q columns: 0, 8 and 16. For 2-CML the zero-run additions are respectively 0, +128M, +256M (sheet 3 T41:T52; sheet 4 T40:T51), where M is the discard-count symbol used by the workbook. CFG_SYNC_LEN at 0x14E says its [15:14] modes are no discard, discard 8 points, discard 16 points (configuration workbook C28/D28/I28). The workbook does not define M as packed decimation E.</FACT>
    <FACT confidence="HIGH">Scenario layouts make the prefix positional: sync_word is followed by an explicit formula-counted *0 run and then indexed channel samples; later formula-counted *0 runs appear between sample groups. Examples: no-DDC integer 2-CML rows 70-71 use sync_word*16, (16N+152)*0, then INx[0..], and (16N-16)*0; DDC rows 130-133 use sync_word*16, (64N+200)*0, then ordered INx_i/INx_q samples and (64N-80)*0. Therefore zero removal is position/configuration based, not a data-value filter.</FACT>
    <FACT confidence="HIGH">The source-wide stated N range is 1..63. The framing tables provide symbolic formulas across that range; they do not state any n=8..24 restriction. Nor do they state that DDC narrows the factor range.</FACT>
